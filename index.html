Profile

Planning the site before the site exists.

This portfolio documents two source-based workshop exercises: planning the logistics and temporary facilities for a seven-block shopping-mall development, and designing SMART-TRACK, a proposed system for traceable construction-material procurement, inspection, storage and accounts.

Supplementary case studies extend these themes into material wastage control, workforce and safety planning, concrete quality management and project monitoring.
